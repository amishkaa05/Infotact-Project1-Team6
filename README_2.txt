EchoChain – Circular Economy & Secondary Market Lifecycle Analytics

Project Overview

EchoChain is a circular economy analytics project that tracks
products through their complete lifecycle, from manufacturing and
customer usage to returns, refurbishment, resale and recycling.

Business Domain

Sustainable Manufacturing & Circular Economy

Technologies Used

* PySpark
* Python
* Microsoft Power BI
* CSV
* GitHub

Key Objectives

- Analyze product lifecycle performance
- Measure circularity rate
- Analyze refurbishment performance
- Measure secondary-market revenue and profit
- Analyze recycling and waste
- Measure carbon savings
- Identify high-performing product categories
